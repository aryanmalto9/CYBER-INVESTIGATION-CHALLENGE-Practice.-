## CYBERCRIME INVESTIGATION BRIEFING DOCUMENT

Issuing Authority: Cyber Police Station – Pimpri Chinchwad, Pune, Maharashtra, India Document Type: Case Briefing / Exercise Dossier Date of Issue: 04 March 2026

## Case Title

Operation Phantom Portfolio

## Case Overview
This dossier outlines a sophisticated, systematic investment fraud operation resulting in a cumulative financial loss of ₹61,64,941. The fraudulent activities occurred between 01 September 2025 and 11 October 2025. The perpetrators utilized social engineering techniques via encrypted messaging platforms and a fabricated financial trading portal to deceive the victim into transferring substantial funds under the guise of highly profitable share market investments.

## Incident Background

The investigation was initiated following a formal complaint registered by the victim through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (NCCRP). Preliminary analysis indicates a highly organized syndicate operating utilizing layered communication channels, spoofed digital infrastructure, and a network of suspected mule accounts to obfuscate the flow of illicitly acquired funds.

## Initial Contact and Fraud Method

The victim was added to an unsolicited WhatsApp group without prior consent. The group was presented as an exclusive forum for professional stock trading guidance and wealth management. The primary modus operandi involved:
1.	Social Proofing: Group administrators and suspected accomplices systematically posted fabricated screenshots depicting massive returns on investments to manufacture credibility.
2.	Authority Bias: The administrators posed as elite financial advisors possessing insider knowledge of the equity markets.
3.	Phishing/Platform Onboarding: The victim was coerced into registering on a third-party application purportedly designed for executing trades, using a targeted referral link.

Trust Building Phase
To mitigate suspicion and solidify the victim's trust, the syndicate permitted the successful processing of two initial withdrawal requests:
•	Withdrawal 1: ₹1,000
•	Withdrawal 2: ₹20,000
These transactions served as a psychological catalyst, convincing the victim of the platform's legitimacy and the liquidity of her funds.
Escalation Phase
Following the successful withdrawals, the suspects applied high-pressure sales tactics. They urged the victim to capitalize on "limited-time" IPO allocations and exclusive block trading opportunities. The "Recharge Manager" continuously supplied the victim with varying domestic bank account details, instructing her to execute immediate NEFT and RTGS transfers to secure her trading positions.
Financial Transactions Overview
•	Execution Period: September 2025 – October 2025
•	Total Capital Transferred: ₹61,64,941
•	Fabricated Portfolio Value: ₹75,00,000 (Displayed as available profit on the fraudulent dashboard)
•	Transaction Methodology: Multiple deposits routed to disparate, unaffiliated beneficiary bank accounts.
Discovery of Fraud
The deception was uncovered when the victim attempted to liquidate the ₹75,00,000 portfolio balance. The withdrawal request was immediately flagged and blocked by the platform. The suspects subsequently informed the victim that the release of funds was contingent upon the payment of exorbitant "IPO subscription charges" and "tax clearance fees." Despite the victim's compliance with initial fee demands, the funds remained inaccessible. The perpetrators subsequently ceased all communication, culminating in the victim's realization of the fraud.

Investigation Assignment
Role: Cyber Financial Investigation Task Force
Participants in this exercise are designated as sworn analysts within the Cyber Financial Investigation Task Force. Your mandate is to conduct a rigorous analysis of the provided evidence, deconstruct the operational methodology of the syndicate, and formulate actionable intelligence.
Investigation Objectives
The Task Force is required to deliver a comprehensive analytical report addressing the following parameters:
•	Modus Operandi Reconstruction: Detail the psychological and technical mechanics utilized to execute the fraud.
•	Financial Tracing: Map the movement of funds from the victim's account through the initial layer of beneficiary accounts.
•	Mule Account Identification: Analyze the transaction patterns to isolate and classify likely money mule accounts utilized by the syndicate.
•	Digital Infrastructure Analysis: Assess the fraudulent domain (stock.velacapitalfund.com) to identify hosting providers, registrars, and potential linkages to other cybercrime campaigns.
•	Actionable Next Steps: Propose specific, legal investigative measures (e.g., Section 91 CrPC notices to banks, IP log requests from ISPs, domain takedown procedures) necessary to identify the physical locations and true identities of the perpetrators.
